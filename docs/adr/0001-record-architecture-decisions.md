# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-10

## Context

Design decisions for robocode-s are made in discussion and need to stay traceable.

## Decision

Significant decisions are recorded as short ADRs in `docs/adr/`, numbered sequentially. `docs/design.md` stays the overall design; ADRs record why a choice was made. A superseded ADR is kept and marked as such.

## Consequences

Every change to a recorded decision needs a new ADR that supersedes the old one.
