# Classic sample robots

The 27 sample robots from classic Robocode 1.11.1 (`sample`, `sampleex`, `samplesentry`, `sampleteam`), copied unmodified from the official installer except for the license header.

- Copyright (c) 2001-2025 Mathew A. Nelson and Robocode contributors.
- Originally distributed under EPL-1.0. Distributed here under EPL-2.0, as permitted by section 7 of EPL-1.0 (see `docs/design.md` §13.1).

They are the first test corpus for the differential tests against classic Robocode, and examples for new robot authors.
