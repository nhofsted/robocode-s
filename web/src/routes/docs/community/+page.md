robocode-s is new, but the Robocode community has been writing, sharing and ranking bots for more than twenty years. Most of what you need to know about building a good bot lives there, and it applies to robocode-s unchanged.

## Learn

- **[RoboWiki](https://robowiki.net/)**: the community's knowledge base since 2003. Movement, targeting, wave surfing, guess factors, a page on almost every well-known bot, and talk pages where you can ask questions. Start with [My First Robot](https://robowiki.net/wiki/Robocode/My_First_Robot) and the [FAQ](https://robowiki.net/wiki/Robocode/FAQ).
- **[The Book of Robocode](https://book.robocode.dev/)**: a guided introduction to the game, its physics and robot strategy.
- **[Classic Robocode API](https://robocode.sourceforge.io/docs/robocode/)**: the Javadoc of the API that classic robots, and robocode-s's Java robots, are written against.

## Compete

- **[LiteRumble](https://rumble.robowiki.net/)**: the current rankings of classic RoboRumble, in 1v1, melee, teams and the weight classes. The [RoboRumble page](https://robowiki.net/wiki/RoboRumble) on the RoboWiki explains how to enter a bot and how to run a client.
- **[robocode-s rumble](/rankings)**: our own rankings, described in [The rumble](/docs/rumble).

## Talk

- **[Robocode Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/robocode)**: the mailing list for questions and announcements.
- **[Robocode on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/robocoders/)**: the Robocode group on Facebook.
- **[r/robocode](https://www.reddit.com/r/robocode/)**: the Robocode subreddit.
- **RoboWiki talk pages**: for discussing a specific bot or technique, the talk page of its wiki page is usually the best place.

## Robocode itself

- **[Robocode](https://robocode.sourceforge.io/)**: the home of classic Robocode, with downloads and documentation. The code is [on GitHub](https://github.com/robo-code/robocode) and news is on the [Robocode blog](https://robo-code.blogspot.com/).
- **[Robocode Tank Royale](https://robocode.dev/)**: the classic team's next generation, with robots in several languages. See [About robocode-s](/docs/about) for how it compares.

## robocode-s

robocode-s is developed [on GitHub](https://github.com/nhofsted/robocode-s). Questions about robocode-s, bug reports and ideas go to its [issues](https://github.com/nhofsted/robocode-s/issues), not to the classic Robocode channels above.
